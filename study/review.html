How it works

  • Each position comes from a 4-player base game with the standard rules. Settlements go down in snake order: seats 1, 2, 3, 4, then 4, 3, 2, 1.
  • The pieces already on the board were placed by a fixed rule, not by real players. Take them as given.
  • Choose the corner you would actually play. Then tick any other corners a strong player could reasonably choose instead, say how sure you are, and add a sentence on why if you like.

  • The page gives no hints and keeps no score. Please do not use Catan Lens or any other tool while you answer.
  • Your answers stay in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. When you are done, download your answers and give the file to the study team.
