Most games of Catan are decided before the first roll, by where you place your first two settlements. Catan Lens helps you get that decision right. Every board follows the official setup. Catan Lens scores every legal corner, and the gold ring marks the strongest one still open. The card beside the board says what that corner produces. Open "Why this corner?" for the reasoning in plain English: how much it yields, which resources, and whether they are rare on this board. Curious about another spot? Click any corner for a grade, its share of the best score, and exactly what it is missing. Keep the best corner instead, and the ring moves to the second settlement that best completes your first. Practise with sixteen drill levels, or copy the real board from your table. It all runs in your browser, with no account and no tracking. Catan Lens. Free, open source, and ready for your next game.
