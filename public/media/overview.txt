Your first settlements shape what you can produce and build. Catan Lens helps you compare those choices before the first roll. Explore a generated board. Catan Lens scores every legal corner, and the gold ring marks the highest score still open. The card beside the board says what that corner produces. Open "Why this corner?" for the reasoning in plain English: how much it yields, which resources, and whether they are rare on this board. Curious about another spot? Click any corner for a grade, its share of the best score, and why another corner scores higher. Keep the best corner instead, and the ring moves to the second settlement that best completes your first. Keep your second settlement to see resource income and the cards your first builds need. Income is an average; actual rolls vary. Practise with sixteen drill levels, or copy the real board from your table. It all runs in your browser, with no account and no tracking. Catan Lens. Free, open source, and ready for your next game.
